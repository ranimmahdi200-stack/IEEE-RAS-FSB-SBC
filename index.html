Who We Are

Empowering Tomorrow's Engineers

IEEE Robotics and Automation Society – FSB Student Branch Chapteris a community of passionate students driven by robotics, automation, embedded systems, artificial intelligence, and emerging technologies. Our chapter brings together students who want to learn, build, innovate, and share knowledge through hands-on projects, technical workshops, competitions, training sessions, and collaborative activities. We believe that robotics is not only about technology — it is about creativity, teamwork, problem-solving, and turning ideas into real-world solutions.

Our Mission

To create an inspiring environment where students can develop their technical and soft skills, explore the world of robotics and automation, and transform their ideas into innovative projects.

Our Vision

To build a strong robotics community that inspires innovation, empowers students, and connects them with the future of technology.
