Cybertron Bootcamp

A curated set of hands-on sessions to build robotics expertise — ROS, embedded systems, CV, IoT and more.

Session 1 — FUNDAMENTALS OF ROBOTICS

Instructor: Mr. Louay Sahbani

Learn the core concepts of robotics from an experienced electrical engineer and former IEEE RAS Chair, and build a strong foundation for the sessions ahead.

Session 2 — The Embedded Blueprint

Instructor: Mr. Mohamed Kada

Learn from an industry expert and strengthen your embedded systems knowledge.

Session 3 — The Language Behind Smart Devices

Instructor: Mr. Mohamed Amine Louati

The Language Behind Smart Devices, where Mr. Mohamed Amine Louati, Embedded Software Engineer at Sagemcom and IEEE RAS Tunisia Section Chapter Secretary, will guide you through the fundamentals of Embedded C and its role in embedded systems.

Session 4 — The IoT Odyssey

Instructor: Mr. Baha Eddine Hammou

M. Baha Eddine Hammou, qui vous guidera à travers le monde de l'Internet des choses, partageant des analyses pratiques et des expériences réelles dans les systèmes embâssés et l'IoT.

Session 5 — COMPUTER VISION FOR ROBOTICS

Instructor: Ms. Imen Masmoudi

Computer Vision for Robotics and discover how robots can see, analyze, and understand the world around them.
